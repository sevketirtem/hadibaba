Kazakh folk instrumental. Lead instrument: Kazakh dombra, the two-stringed long-necked Central Asian lute with gut strings: dry, bright, twangy, percussive tone, played with rapid "kui" strumming (quick down-up wrist strokes, hammering rhythmic drive on two strings in fourths). It must NOT sound like a guitar, mandolin or banjo: no guitar at all, no piano, no synth. Accompaniment: only a light frame drum (dabyl) and a few hand claps. Steppe, Central Asian, Kazakh wedding-dance energy; playful, cheeky, confident mood, like two friends teasing each other before a football match. About 118 BPM. Open with a strong, instantly recognizable 2-bar dombra hook in the first 3 seconds, repeat the hook, develop it, and end with a clear final strum. Instrumental only, absolutely no vocals, no singing, no spoken words. Original melody; do not quote any existing song or folk tune. About 40 seconds long.
