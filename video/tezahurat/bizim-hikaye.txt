Joyful, romantic Turkish football terrace chant that feels like a wedding celebration, mid-tempo, about 120 seconds. Thousands of fans singing together, one big davul drum, hand claps, stadium echo; in the chorus a call-and-response like a Kazakh wedding: one voice asks "Jarasında jippa?" and the whole crowd shouts back "Jarasında jıpp!". A warm male voice leads the verses, a woman's voice sings the Kazakh lines softly ("Janım", "Seni süyemin", "Biz birgemiz"), then the crowd joins. Mostly Turkish with a few Kazakh words; pronounce the Kazakh words the Kazakh way, exactly as written. Use exactly these lyrics, do not add other words. No real club names.
